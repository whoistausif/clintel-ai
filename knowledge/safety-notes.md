# Research-use safety limitations

Use only approved, deidentified data and comply with dataset terms. Keep patient-level information out of public repositories, public logs, screenshots, and public deployments. Use authentication, authorization, encryption in transit, least-privilege database roles, secret management, strict file validation, and audit logging for a governed deployment.

Model explanations represent feature associations with an output and are not causal conclusions. An evidence assistant must cite its permitted sources, say when evidence is unavailable, and must not invent medical guidance. ClinIntel AI is not for autonomous diagnosis or treatment and does not replace qualified clinicians.
