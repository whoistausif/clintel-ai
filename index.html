PROJECT OVERVIEW

About ClinIntel AI.

A research prototype for clinical risk prediction, explainability and evidence-grounded information retrieval.

✳

Clinical intelligence,
with context.

Clinical data spans structured measurements and narrative information. ClinIntel AI explores how a patient timeline, clearly defined prediction targets, interpretable model outputs and retrieved evidence could be brought together in a research interface.

Risk predictionTemporal dataExplainable MLClinical NLPRAG
Intended use

Research and education only. The prototype does not diagnose, prescribe, triage, or replace a qualified clinician.
